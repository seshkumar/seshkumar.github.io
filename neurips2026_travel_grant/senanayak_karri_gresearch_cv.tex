% ---------- Research Summary ----------
\section{Research Summary}
\small
Machine-learning researcher at Imperial College London specialising in probabilistic modelling, graph-structured learning, geometry-aware neural networks, and convex and submodular optimisation. PhD in machine learning from INRIA / \'Ecole Normale Sup\'erieure (SIERRA), followed by research appointments in statistical machine learning and data science. Two papers accepted for poster presentation at NeurIPS 2026, on non-stationary graph Gaussian processes and learning neural-network edge geometry through Banach duality. Research published at NeurIPS, ICML, JMLR, TMLR, and \textit{Machine Learning}. Finance is an application domain for my methodological work: I study learning under non-stationarity, structured high-dimensional estimation, and reinforcement learning for sequential decisions, with applications to financial time series, covariance estimation, and portfolio management.
\normalsize
