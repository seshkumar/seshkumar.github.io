I have two papers accepted for poster presentation at NeurIPS 2026, published under my research name, K. S. Sesh Kumar: (1) Practical Non-Stationary Graph Gaussian Processes — first author; submission ID 25512. This paper develops Gaussian-process models for heterogeneous graphs, linking node-level variability to interaction strength through an interpretable parameter. The contribution concerns probabilistic modelling of graph-structured data with variation across nodes. (2) Geometry-Constrained Kolmogorov–Arnold Networks: Learning Edge Geometry via Banach Duality — sole author. This paper introduces learnable edge functions derived from Banach-space duality, allowing each edge’s geometry to adapt to the data. It studies interpretable representations and learning with noisy or limited observations. Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25807. Attending would allow me to present both contributions, discuss them with the probabilistic modelling and neural-network research communities, and develop research collaborations.
