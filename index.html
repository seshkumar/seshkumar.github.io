Research Fellow

Brevan Howard Centre for Financial Analysis

I am a research fellow at the Brevan Howard Centre for Financial Analysis, Imperial College Business School working with Prof. Allen Franklin since Sept, 2022. Prior to this, I was a research fellow at the Data Science Institute, Imperial College London from Sept 2019 to Aug 2022. Earlier from Dec 2017 to Aug 2019, my fellowship was funded by the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence (CFI) at Imperial College London working with Prof. Marc Deisenroth and Dr. Adrian Weller. I was a post-doctoral researcher in the group of Vladimir Kolmogorov at IST, Austria until October, 2017. I finished my PhD under the supervision of Francis Bach at INRIA, SIERRA in September, 2016. I received MVA Masters from ENS-Cachan in 2013 and Bachelors degree (Computer Science and Engineering) from IIIT, Hyderabad in 2003.

My research revolves around exploring the links between different topics in the following areas :
  • Evolutionary Markets in Finance
  • Discrete optimization
  • Machine learning
